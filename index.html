The Coach

Your blackjack coach. Deal a hand and I'll help you decide whether to take a card or stop — and explain why, in plain English.

Why reshuffle every hand?

Card counters track the ratio of high to low cards left in the shoe. Shuffling a full deck after every hand keeps that ratio pinned at neutral — the count is always zero, so there's no edge to grind. Play the math, not the memory.

The rules on this table

Single deck, dealer stands on all 17s, blackjack pays 3:2. Double on any first two cards, double after split, split up to four hands. No insurance — it's a sucker bet, so we skip it.

How the coach helps

It uses basic strategy — the best move for your cards against the dealer's shown card, worked out by running the odds. Set it to Before to see the move up front, After to test yourself and get graded, or Off. It never peeks at the dealer's face-down card.

Play money only. This is a strategy trainer, not a casino — and the house still keeps a small edge even with perfect play.
