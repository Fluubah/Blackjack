The Coach

Basic strategy, live. Deal a hand and I'll tell you the mathematically correct move against the dealer's up-card — and exactly why it's right.

Why reshuffle every hand?

Card counters track the ratio of high to low cards left in the shoe. Shuffling a full deck after every hand keeps that ratio pinned at neutral — the count is always zero, so there's no edge to grind. Play the math, not the memory.

The rules on this table

Single deck, dealer stands on all 17s, blackjack pays 3:2. Double on any first two cards, double after split, split up to four hands. No insurance — it's a sucker bet, so we skip it.

How the coach thinks

Every call comes from basic strategy: the move with the best expected value for your total against the dealer's up-card. It never peeks at the hole card — same information you have.
